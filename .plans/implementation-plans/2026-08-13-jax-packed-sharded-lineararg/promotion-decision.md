## Blockers and retained fallbacks

Promotion is blocked by:

1. every required product packed/exact warm ratio exceeding 1.20 in both fresh and reused cache states;
2. missing x86_64 CPU evidence;
3. missing GPU evidence; and
4. runner metadata `promotable=false` because collection used `--no-enforce-gates` to finish both failing cache states.

The packed implementation is numerically and structurally viable on the available arm64 CPU environment, so the evidence does not justify `reject`. It also cannot justify `promote`. The retained exact-ragged and NumPy/Cython paths provide tested fallbacks while performance and platform blockers remain.

A future promotion attempt must collect a clean, enforced run at one candidate commit and dataset fingerprint on arm64 CPU, x86_64 CPU, forced two-device CPU, and GPU, with every required product ratio at or below 1.20. Even then, public class renaming, export routing, compatibility migration, and CLI routing require a separate maintainer-approved implementation plan.
